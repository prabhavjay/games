Subnautica 2 Bio-Scan Log

Lifeforms and other targets that count toward your bio-scan total. That total unlocks extra passive biomod slots at the Biolab. Tick each one after you scan it with the Bioscanner; your ticks stay in this browser.

Bio-scans 0 / 80 Next passive slot at 10
  • Use the BioscannerScans from the regular Scanner don't count. Craft the Bioscanner at the Modification Station: Scanner, 3 Conduit Crystal, 2 Enameled Glass.
  • Each lifeform counts onceScanning the same species again adds nothing. Check your running total at a Biolab.
  • Slot milestonesYou start with 1 passive slot. More open at 10, 20, 40 and 80 bio-scans, for 5 in total.
Not all 80No public source lists all 80 yet. This page has the 0 targets I could confirm: 37 from the community bio-scan table plus extras that players report counting. Players say about 76 is reachable without much trouble and the last few are obscure. The rest are tracked in the community's missing-scans spreadsheet.
About 30 bio-scans not listed hereGuides I could reach don't name them. Players point to small flora, fragments of plants and corals, and hidden objects as the usual gaps. Scan any lifeform you haven't seen in the Biolab total, and check the 80 Bio Scans and “Is there actually 80?” Steam threads.
