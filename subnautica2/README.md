# Subnautica 2 Bio-Scan Log

A single-page checklist of things you can scan with the Bioscanner in Subnautica 2. Your bio-scan total unlocks extra passive biomod slots at 10, 20, 40 and 80 scans.

Open `bioscan-log.html` in a browser. Ticks are saved in that browser's local storage.

The data comes from community guides and player threads (Early Access). It lists 49 of the 80 scans; sources are linked in the page footer.
